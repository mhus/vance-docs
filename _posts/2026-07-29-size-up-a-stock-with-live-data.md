# Size up a stock with live data

Ask a real question — *"how's this stock doing, and what do you make of it?"* —
and Vance does the whole thing in one message: it recognises it needs *live*
data, spawns a research worker to pull the figures off the web, draws them as a
native chart, and writes an assessment that **cites the data it's standing on**.

We asked it about Apple's last ten years. It fetched the yearly prices, rendered
a **candlestick chart** (honestly noting the current year is incomplete), and then
gave a verdict with its reasons on the table — the 12.5× decade, only two down
years, the slowing pace, the ~30–33× valuation.

![A candlestick chart of AAPL's yearly prices]({{ '/assets/img/howtos/uc-stock/chart.png' | relative_url }}){: .doc-shot }

That's the difference from a chatbot answer: real data it looked up itself, a
chart that's a real editable document, and a view you can argue with because the
reasons are shown.
